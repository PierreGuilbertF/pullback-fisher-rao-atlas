# Pullback Fisher–Rao Atlas

Code, numerical experiments, and reusable figures accompanying the paper

**“Pullback Fisher–Rao Geometries of Parametric Probability Families: An Expository Atlas”**

by **Pierre Guilbert**.

---

## About

This repository accompanies an expository study of the Fisher–Rao geometry
induced on finite-dimensional parameter spaces by pulling back the
Fisher–Rao metric from the space of probability densities.

The paper applies the same geometric construction to several classes of
parametric probability models:

- univariate Gaussian distributions;
- multivariate Gaussian distributions;
- univariate and multivariate elliptical Laplace distributions;
- linear Gibbs families;
- nonlinear Gibbs families;
- push-forward families generated by smooth transformations of a reference
  probability distribution.

For each family, the parameter-to-density map is differentiated, the
corresponding pullback Fisher–Rao metric is derived, and its geometric
meaning is analyzed.

Particular attention is given to the interpretation of the metric in terms
of location, scale, anisotropy, orientation, energy fluctuations, and local
mass compression, as well as to the geometry of the resulting geodesics.

When closed-form geodesics are not available, numerical approximations are
obtained by minimizing a discrete Riemannian energy.

The paper also discusses the infinitesimal contrast between Fisher–Rao and
Wasserstein–Otto geometry.
